A retail dashboard the owner opens Mondays.

Most dashboards die in a shared drive. This one survived because it answers exactly three questions — and nothing else.

The pipeline

From spreadsheet chaos to one screen.

01

Collect

POS exports and Excel sheets unified into one table with a shared date key.

02

Model

Star schema in Power BI: one fact table, clean dimension tables.

03

Measure

DAX measures for revenue, growth vs last year, and top-region share.

04

Design

Three questions, one screen each — filters that a non-analyst can use.

05

Hand over

A 10-minute walkthrough video so the owner never needs me again.

The question

Where is growth really coming from — by quarter, by region — and what should we double down on?

The data

Synthetic sample of quarterly revenue across 3 regions, modeled on typical small-retail POS exports.

The method

Power BI star schema + DAX: year-over-year growth and region share as the two headline measures. Every visual answers one of the three agreed questions.

The result

Q4 in the North region drives 41% of annual growth in the sample — the stocking decision for next year writes itself.

Sample  Filter by region — the chart re-renders from the embedded sample dataset. Figures in $k, illustrative.

Caveats — stated upfront

  • Synthetic sample data — no real store, no real revenue.
  • A real engagement starts by agreeing the three questions with the owner before a single visual is built.
  • Dashboards shown here are rebuilt as live web charts; a client Power BI file ships with the .pbix and a walkthrough.
