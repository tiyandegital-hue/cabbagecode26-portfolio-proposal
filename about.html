I'm [Analyst Name], a data analyst working in Excel, Power BI, and SQL — with Python where the problem earns it.

I started on the operations side of a business, building the spreadsheets everyone else complained about. The complaints were always the same: the numbers existed, but nobody could decide anything from them. So I learned to close that gap — from raw extract to a one-sentence answer.

My rule for every engagement: if you can't explain the finding to a non-analyst in thirty seconds, the analysis isn't finished. That means cleaning logs you can audit, metrics chosen for the decision (not for vanity), and caveats stated before anyone asks.

I work with founders, small teams, and hiring managers who'd rather see one honest pipeline than ten polished screenshots. The live demo on the home page is the fastest way to see how I think.

How I work

Boring process, interesting results.

STEP 1

Define the question

We agree on the decision the analysis must support — in writing, before data is touched.

STEP 2

Clean in the open

Every fix logged: dedupes, imputations, outliers. You see the mess and what I did about it.

STEP 3

Answer plainly

One headline finding, the evidence behind it, and the caveats — no jargon wall.

STEP 4

Hand over

Files, queries, and a walkthrough so your team can rerun everything without me.
