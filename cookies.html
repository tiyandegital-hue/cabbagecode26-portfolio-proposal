Cookie Policy

This site sets no cookies. There is no analytics, no advertising, no embedded third-party content — so there is nothing to consent to, and no consent banner.

Why no banner?

Cookie banners exist to disclose tracking. With zero tracking, a banner would be theater. This page is the disclosure instead — plain, permanent, and checkable in your browser's dev tools.

If that changes

Should the live site ever add analytics or embeds, this policy will name each one, explain what it does, and a real consent banner will appear before anything loads.
