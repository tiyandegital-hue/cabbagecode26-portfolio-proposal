Privacy Policy

The short version: this demo site collects nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no cookies, no third-party requests. Your visit leaves no trace on our end.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no analytics software, no tracking pixel, and no server-side logging of individual visits beyond what any web host records by default (and this demo has no backend at all).

The contact form

The form on the contact page is a preview: pressing "Send message" only shows a demo confirmation in your browser. Nothing is transmitted, stored, or emailed anywhere. On the live site, the policy will state exactly where messages go and how long they're kept.

Third parties

None are loaded. Fonts are self-hosted, charts are drawn by code on this page, and there are no embeds (maps, videos, social widgets). The only outbound link to a third party is the optional Threads DM link, which opens in your own app.

Your rights

Since no personal data is collected, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. If that changes on the live site, this policy will be updated first — not after.
